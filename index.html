A calm, three-step clinical pathway

No jargon, no black boxes. Every prediction arrives with a visual reason a clinician can verify in seconds.

①

Capture

Any standard fundus camera or smartphone adapter. JPG, JPEG and PNG accepted up to 4K.

②

Explain

Grad-CAM heatmaps highlight microaneurysms, haemorrhages and exudates driving the grade.

③

Act

Referral urgency is routed to the doctor portal with a printable, audit-ready summary.

Explainability is the diagnosis

RETINOVA never asks you to trust a number. Each grade is paired with lesion-level evidence, a confidence interval and a plain-language rationale you can share with the patient.
